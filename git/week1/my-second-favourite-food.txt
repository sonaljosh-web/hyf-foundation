Chicken Curry

Ingredients:
- 300g chicken
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tablespoon curry powder
- 400ml coconut milk
- 1 tablespoon cooking oil
- Salt and pepper

Instructions:
1. Cut the chicken into small pieces.
2. Fry the onion and garlic in the oil.
3. Add the chicken and cook until browned.
4. Add the curry powder and stir.
5. Pour in the coconut milk.
6. Simmer for 15 minutes.
7. Season with salt and pepper and serve with rice.
