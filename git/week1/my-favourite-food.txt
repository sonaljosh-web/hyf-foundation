Spaghetti Bolognese

Ingredients:
- 250g spaghetti
- 250g minced beef
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 400g chopped tomatoes
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Salt and pepper

Instructions:
1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
2. Heat the olive oil and fry the onion and garlic.
3. Add the minced beef and cook until browned.
4. Add the chopped tomatoes, salt, and pepper.
5. Simmer for 15 minutes.
6. Serve the sauce with the spaghetti.
